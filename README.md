**Investigation of Magnetic Field Effects on Electrochemical Energy Storage Performance**

----------------------------------------

Recipient of Top Project Award at the Toronto Regional Science Fair (330+ projects, https://www.torontosciencefair.ca/previous-fairs/2024-fair/2024-tsf-awards-ceremony/2024-special-award-recipients).

• Conducted an experimental investigation of magnetic-field effects on large-scale renewable energy storage systems.
• Achieved a 3.3x increase in electrochemical reaction rate, boosting theoretical round-trip efficiency from 70% to 83%.
• Demonstrated that magnetic-field enhancement improves charge-transfer kinetics while preserving the cost, safety, and scalability of iron-based flow batteries.
• Expanded research through computational modeling of lithium-ion cells, validating the broader potential of magnetic-assisted electrochemistry across varying battery chemistries.
